About Aero Arcade & Safety Guide

Welcome to Aero Arcade 🫧

Step back into the optimistic, glossy web era of the late 2000s. Aero Arcade is a lightweight collection of browser-based mini-games designed for quick, relaxing breaks—no downloads, no accounts, and zero clutter.

How It Works
  • Instant Play: Select any active title from the Arcade Shelf to load it directly.
  • Local Progress: Your high scores save right inside your browser automatically.
100% Safe & Private
  • Zero Installations: Runs entirely in your browser via HTML5/JS.
  • No Data Tracking: No emails, passwords, or personal data collected. Securely hosted via HTTPS.
